spine. The story spine was pioneered by the Pixar company and it's a way to distill a story into

story and puts details into the story. But because he has a structure, he is able to push the
plot in the correct direction.

So this is a story spine. It's made up of six prompts and it starts off with:
  Once upon a time…
  Every day…
  One day…
  Because of that…
  Because of that…
  Until finally…

This is the format you want to be able to fit your story into, in order to consolidate it and add
detail and scenes to expand it.
