## Operating rules

1. Deliver with the boring stack you already know — no learning new tools on client time. New tech happens in the Deep Track only.
2. Everything reusable goes into private template repos → each delivery gets faster → effective hourly rate climbs without raising the time cap.
3. Raise prices ~20% after every 2 completed projects. Your public blog/GitHub becomes sales collateral for free.
4. Say no to custom scope. "That's a great idea — it's outside this package" is a complete sentence.
5. Revenue target by month 3: enough that money stress doesn't leak into Deep Track hours. That's the entire KPI.
