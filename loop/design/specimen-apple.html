Conditional Probability

Lecture quiz · this lecture only3 of 3

A and B are disjoint, with P(A) and P(B) both above 0. Are they independent?

Not quite. Knowing A occurred tells you B certainly did not, which is the most informative thing one event can say about another. This one comes back at the end.
