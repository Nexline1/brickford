Summary

The idea

A linear transformation is fully described by where it sends the basis vectors î and ĵ.

Worked

If î ↦ (1, −2) and ĵ ↦ (3, 0), then (−1, 2) ↦ −1·(1, −2) + 2·(3, 0) = (5, 2).
