# Owner's reference images (4 Nov 2026)

Two Dribbble-style app mockups the owner sent as the *feel* to aim for. They sit inside the blueprint
(midnight #12141C canvas, white sheet with 36px top corners); they do not replace it.

## ref-aura-fit.webp ("Aura Fit")
- Soft frosted gradient surfaces: white fading to pale grey-green, never flat grey.
- One deep-green brand colour for the dark cards and selected states; a brighter green gradient for the one primary action.
- Friendly large greeting header ("Good morning, Parker / Ready to train?") with the second line in a soft grey.
- Selectable chips: fully rounded, dark filled when selected, white with a hairline when not. Wrapped in a loose cloud.
- Primary button: gradient pill, UPPERCASE letter-spaced label, small "+" icon. Secondary: outlined pill. Tiny uppercase footer links.
- A dark green card with white text holding a video thumbnail and a round play button (this is the lecture card).
- Glowing orb as the brand mark. A frosted input bar with small round icon buttons.

## ref-booktok.webp ("Booktok finds")
- Clean white screens. A horizontal filter-chip row at the top ("All" selected, light grey).
- Hero carousel: one big centred card with its neighbours peeking in from the sides; its one-line blurb and a dark "Save" pill below.
- Section header: bold title left, "See more" right.
- Floating frosted pill tab bar (icon + tiny label per tab, selected tab has a soft pill behind it) with a separate round search button beside it.
- Bottom sheet with a drag handle and list rows: thumbnail, bold title, grey subtitle, a light-grey rounded-square bookmark button, a dark "View" pill button.
- Full-bleed media with frosted round overlay buttons (back, more).
